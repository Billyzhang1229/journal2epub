## The articles are not covered by it

Editions built with this software contain journal articles that are not the
software's to relicense. Each article:

* remains under **its own open-access licence** (most commonly CC BY) and the
  copyright of its own rights holders;
* is reproduced with its authors, original citation, DOI and licence terms
  attached, automatically, in an attribution block at the end of the article.

If you redistribute an edition, you are redistributing those articles under
their own terms, not under the MIT licence.

## Editions are unofficial

Editions produced by this software are independently produced and are **not**
published by, endorsed by, or affiliated with any publisher. They deliberately
carry no publisher logo, wordmark or trade dress, and every edition says so on
its title page and in its colophon.

National Library of Medicine. Per NLM's terms of use, NLM is acknowledged as
the source of the data, and no PMC wordmark or logo is used. NLM does not
